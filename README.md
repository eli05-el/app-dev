# My Favorite Movie: *The Croods*

## Overview
*The Croods* is a **family adventure comedy** produced by **DreamWorks Animation**.  
It tells the story of a prehistoric family who must leave their cave and explore a dangerous but beautiful new world.

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Main Characters
- **Grug (Nicolas Cage)** – The overprotective father who struggles to adapt to change.  
- **Eep (Emma Stone)** – A curious and brave teenage daughter who dreams of exploring.  
- **Guy (Ryan Reynolds)** – A clever young man who brings new ideas and inventions.  
- **Ugga (Catherine Keener)** – The caring and supportive mother.  
- **Gran (Cloris Leachman)** – The tough, funny grandmother who always surprises everyone.  

---

### 💬 Favorite Quote
> “The only way to survive is if the pack stays together.” — Grug

### 🦕 Fun Facts
- The Croods was **nominated for an Academy Award** for Best Animated Feature.  
- It was one of the first DreamWorks films made with new 3D animation technology.  
- The movie teaches about **family, adaptation, and embracing change**.

- It’s **funny, emotional, and visually stunning**.
